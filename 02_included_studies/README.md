# Included studies

- management_reviews.csv: the 32 management reviews (P1 to P32) that form the RQ1 corpus, with title, authors and year. The P-codes are the ones cited in Table I of the paper as the sources of the six added attributes, and they are the column headers of 04_coding/coding_matrix_management.csv.
- se_reviews.csv: the 34 software engineering reviews (P1 to P34) that form the RQ2 corpus, with title, authors and year. The P-codes are the column headers of 04_coding/coding_matrix_se.csv.
- P23 in se_reviews.csv carries a 2014 publication year although the search window starts in 2015; it was retained because it was returned by the search and met the inclusion criteria.
